I am a first-year M.S. student at the Institute of Software, Chinese Academy of Sciences (ISCAS), working in the VerSys group at the Key Laboratory of System Software, advised by Prof. Fu Song. Before that, I received my B.Sc. from the Kuang Yaming Honors School, Nanjing University.

My research centers on formal verification — building rigorous, machine-checked arguments that software and hardware systems behave as intended. I am broadly drawn to theoretical computer science, especially the logic, automata, and semantics that make such guarantees possible.

Research Interests

Formal Verification

My primary interest. I aim to build rigorous, machine-checked guarantees about real systems — from program logics and model checking to the verification of foundational software such as compilers and operating systems.

Theoretical Computer Science

I enjoy the mathematics that underpins verification: mathematical logic, automata theory, formal semantics, and complexity — the theory that turns correctness into a theorem.
